What this tool does

Video Autopilot is a private tool used only by the channel owner. It uploads the owner's own finished videos to the owner's own YouTube, Instagram and TikTok accounts, and reads those accounts' analytics so future episodes can be improved. It has no public users and does not access anyone else's account.
