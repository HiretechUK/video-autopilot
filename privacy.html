Privacy Policy

Last updated 30 October 2026

Video Autopilot ("the tool") is a private publishing tool operated by Haseeb Ahmed for the channels Imagine You and Paws & Claws Club. This policy explains what data the tool handles.

Who uses the tool

Only the channel owner. The tool is not offered to the public, and no one else can sign in to it.

Data the tool accesses

  • YouTube (via YouTube API Services): permission to upload videos, set titles, descriptions and thumbnails, and read channel and video analytics, for the owner's own channels only.
  • Instagram (via the Instagram API): permission to publish the owner's own Reels and posts and read their insights.
  • TikTok (via the TikTok Content Posting API): permission to publish the owner's own videos and read basic account information.

Data the tool does not collect

The tool does not collect, store or process personal data from viewers, followers or any other users. It does not use cookies, tracking or advertising identifiers. Paws & Claws Club content is made for children and is published as "made for kids"; the tool collects no data from or about children.

How access tokens are stored

Access tokens issued by Google, Meta and TikTok are stored encrypted on the owner's private server and are used only to perform the actions above. They are never sold, shared or transferred to third parties.

Google API Services

The tool's use and transfer of information received from Google APIs adheres to the Google API Services User Data Policy, including the Limited Use requirements. By using YouTube features you agree to the YouTube Terms of Service, and Google's handling of data is described in the Google Privacy Policy. Access can be revoked at any time at Google security settings.

Retention and deletion

Analytics data is kept only as long as needed to plan future episodes. Revoking access on any platform stops the tool immediately; stored tokens and data are deleted on request.
